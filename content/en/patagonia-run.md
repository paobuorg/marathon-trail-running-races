## What this race is

- Location: San Martín de los Andes, Neuquén, Argentina. The regulations state the race takes place inside Parque Nacional Lanín and on the surrounding hills and trails.
- Identity: Patagonia Run is a South American long-distance trail event. The organiser calls the 100 Millas “la distancia madre” (the mother distance) and rates its difficulty “Alta”.
- Shape of the week: five days and six races, from the 21k on Wednesday to the 70k on Saturday. The 100 Millas and the 110k both start on Friday.
- Start: RCM4. The 2027 page gives the start venue and date but no finish address. The 2026 schedule page lists the finish at Plaza San Martín (Av. San Martín y Tte. Cnel. Pérez); that is a reference point, not confirmed 2027 information.
- Terrain: the official text names the national park and surrounding trails only. There is no 2027 point table; the 2026 guide gives a 20-row point table and eight intermediate cut-offs, while the 2026 profile gives named summits and an approximate high point (below). Both are 2026 baselines, not promises for 2027.

## Why this page is thinner than others

This is not simply “less material”. Three different causes sit behind it, and they are kept separate.

### 1. Officially withheld: GPX and the course track

This is the organiser's policy, not missing data. The official 2026 technical briefing (42k session) says:

> “No hay tracks GPS oficiales”
> “La navegación es por marcación de carrera.”
> “No se comparten tracks del circuito.”

The same document repeats later: “no se compartirán tracks GPS oficiales. La carrera se sigue por marcación y por lo anunciado por Dirección Técnica.”. It also states that private, military, institutional and community land is authorised **only on the official race date and during the official hours**; entering before or after the event to reconnoitre or train can jeopardise future permits and may lead to sanctions up to disqualification.

That explains the absence: the organiser does not forget to post a track — it chooses not to publish one and requires navigation by course markings. Neither the 2027 100 Millas page nor the 2027 regulations provide a GPX.

This page therefore **shows no track and does not reconstruct one** from text descriptions.

### 2. Not yet published: expected before race day

- **Checkpoint distances and cut-offs**: the 2027 guide and technical briefing are not published; the 2026 guide's 20-row PAS/PAEX table and eight intermediate cut-offs are shown above as a **2026 baseline**, not confirmed 2027 data.
- **Start time**: the 100 Millas page says “Hora: A definir”.
- **Mandatory kit**: Article 7, “ELEMENTOS OBLIGATORIOS”, consists of one sentence: “Próximamente más información”.
- **Bib-pickup venue**: the regulations say “lugar a confirmar”.
- **Rules for the 100 Millas outside assistant**: the regulations defer them to the *Guía del Corredor de 100Mi*, which is not published.

These are cases where the organiser says the information will come, but it has not arrived yet — unlike the permanent policy above.

### 3. Not verified by us: material we could not read

- **Which Hardrock edition a 2027 finish qualifies for**: Hardrock's official qualifying-race list is a Google Sheets page that could not be read in this round. Only the race organiser's own wording is used; nothing is inferred.

- **Travel, accommodation and shuttles**: official travel pages were not checked in this round.

## Entry and fees

- **Process**: the 100 Millas is not a direct registration. You must first “POSTULATE” with supporting evidence; the organising committee reviews it and authorises registration.
- **Standards (2027 regulations)**, any one of:
  1. At least 12 ITRA points from no more than 3 races between **2025 and 2027**, including at least one non-stop race of 110 km or more;
  2. A 2025 or 2026 Patagonia Run 110k finish inside the official cut-off, or a 2025/2026 Patagonia Run 100 Millas finish;
  3. A finish between 2025 and 2027 in another comparable non-stop race, in distance and elevation, with results links submitted for committee review.

This page follows the **regulations**, because they are the operative document for 2027 entry and were updated later than the promotional page; the 2024–2026 text looks like an unrevised carry-over. Both versions are shown; neither is averaged or blended.

- **A drafting error in the regulations**: the Spanish text says “one of the following **four**” (uno de los cuatro) but lists only three; the English version says “one of the following criteria” and lists the same three. Read it as three.
- **Fees (Etapa 7 current)**:
  - Argentine residents: ARS 1,199,900 in one payment; 1,270,500 over three; 1,380,000 over six.
  - Foreign runners: USD 782 in one payment; USD 821.10 over three. Foreign runners have only the one- or three-payment option.
  - The organiser states the total is 20% entry fee and 80% services, fees, taxes and charges; the payment platform adds a 6% service charge.
- **Discounts**: 10% for Running Team members; 30% for residents of San Martín de los Andes and Junín de los Andes; 12%–75% loyalty discounts from five editions. None are cumulative.
- **Distance changes**: self-service in the runner profile until 2027-03-28; no admin fee until 2027-01-31, then “monto a definir” from 1 February to 28 March; moving down a distance refunds no difference.
- **Withdrawal**: within 10 calendar days of signing up, a refund may be requested under Article 34 of the consumer protection law; after that there is no refund and no transfer to another person. Non-participation can earn credit toward the **2028** edition: 100% if notified by 28 February, 75% from 1 to 31 March, and case-by-case from 1 April.
- **Organiser cancellation**: if the race is cancelled for public safety, weather, pandemic, health or force majeure, the entry is carried to the next edition and the fee is not refunded.

## Bib pickup and pre-race

- **How**: in person, by appointment; no proxy collection. Scheduled on the day before each distance's start, 6 to 9 April 2027, in San Martín de los Andes, at a venue still to be confirmed.
- **Booking**: appointments open in the runner profile from the last week of March 2027.

## Kit

Article 7 of the 2027 regulations says only “Próximamente más información”. **No 2027 mandatory kit list has been published.**

The 2026 100Mi guide's mandatory kit (**not the 2027 requirement**) was:

- Two head torches or hand lights of at least **200 lumens** each, with batteries for **30 hours**; one must be worn and switched on at the start, worn after 17:00 and switched on from 18:45 to 08:00.
- A whistle, survival blanket, at least **1.5 L** water capacity, a personal cup, a ziplock bag for rubbish, and warm layers (long trousers, windproof jacket, fleece, gloves and hat).
- No disposable cups are provided; carry your own cup for cold and hot drinks.
- The bib must stay visible on the front, and kit can be checked on course; missing items can lead to disqualification.

The 2026 42k technical briefing is a different distance in a different year and cannot replace the 100Mi list. The 2027 list remains subject to the official guide.
