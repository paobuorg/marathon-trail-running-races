## What this race is

- Location: Paraty, Rio de Janeiro state, Brazil. Start and finish are both in the historic centre (Centro Histórico).
- Status: a UTMB World Series event. PTR 100 sits in the 100K category, awards 3 Running Stones and the page lists Finals Access.
- Route: historic centre → Taquari → Serraria → Pedra da Macela → Cachoeira 7 Quedas → historic centre. The official overview names Taquari, Pedra da Macela and Caminho do Ouro. Pedra da Macela, at 1,797 m in the point table, is the highest point.

![A stone section of the Caminho do Ouro near Paraty: moss-covered blocks, fallen leaves and Atlantic Forest on both sides; not the 2027 race or an aid station](/media/routes/paraty-brazil/paraty-caminho-do-ouro-2007.jpg)

It is not confirmed as the exact 2027 race section and is not race-day photography.*

- Shape: the first 32 km climb slowly near sea level; the biggest climb comes between 32 and 51 km (+1,825 m, up to 1,254 m); the course reaches 1,797 m at 79.9 km, then drops roughly 1,800 m over the last 22 km back to the coast.

- Edition split: the 2026 edition was branded **PTR 108**, its live header showed 67.1 miles (108 km nominal) and 15,912 ft+, and its race report gives an organiser measurement of 101.9 km / +4,778 m. The 2027 edition is **PTR 100**, with a 101 km header and a 102.2 km point table. The name is not the measured distance; this page keeps the three figures side by side.

## Four distance figures

The official header and FAQ both give **101 km / 4,850 m+**. The point table embedded in the same official page ends at **102,215 m**, with point-to-point gain summing to **+4,778 m / −4,781 m**. The card uses the headline figure and the table reproduces the point data. Neither number is rewritten or averaged; the organiser does not explain the difference.

The third figure is the local measurement: the official linked 2026 GPX has 7,974 points after de-duplication, a haversine distance of **101.104 km**, a low point of 3 m and a high point of 1,800 m; raw point-by-point change is **+5,258 / −5,258 m**, and a 3 m threshold reduces it to **+2,713 / −2,726 m**. A single-file point sum also amplifies elevation noise and cannot replace the official +4,778 m. The 2026 race report gives yet another organiser measurement of **101.9 km / +4,778 m**. The four figures (101 header, 102.2 table, 101.1 GPX, 101.9 previous-edition measurement) are kept side by side; this page does not invent an "average course".

## Aid, outside support and drop bags

- **Outside support**: for PTR 100 it is allowed only at Taquari (32.3 km) and Trutario Rio Manso (71.6 km). Every other checkpoint and location is off limits. PTR 55, 34, 25, 17, NIGHT 10 and RUN 7 prohibit outside support entirely.
- **Drop bags**: PTR 100 runners get two, available at Taquari and Trutario Rio Manso. Collection at the finish closes at 12:00 on 26 September 2027; uncollected bags are donated or discarded. Drop-bag rules for the other races were not verified in this round.
- **Cut-off rule**: the clock is the time you leave a main aid station. The five published times are those stations' closings; changes are announced at the briefing or by text message.

## Kit

The official requirements come in three sets; the basic set applies throughout:

- **Basic kit (mandatory)**: trail pack; personal cup of at least 150 ml; phone with LiveTrail installed (airplane mode is prohibited; a power bank is recommended); at least 1 L of water; two head torches with spare batteries for each; waterproof hooded jacket (at least 10,000 Schmerber, RET < 13, sealed seams); survival blanket of at least 1.40 × 2 m; whistle; self-adhesive elastic bandage of at least 100 cm × 6 cm; cap or bandana; photo ID.
- **Basic kit (recommended)**: at least one gel or bar per estimated hour.
- **Hot-weather kit**: SPF 50+ sunscreen; at least 2 L of water; sunglasses and a desert cap recommended.
- **Cold-weather kit**: waterproof jacket; a warm second layer of at least 180 g for a men's M (or a 110 g warm layer plus a water-repellent wind jacket); warm hat; a third fleece layer; trail shoes (minimalist shoes are not accepted); spare warm clothing recommended.

The organiser lists “at least one gel or bar per hour” under both mandatory and recommended kit, which is inconsistent. Prepare it as mandatory.

- **Where**: the EXPO. The FAQ did not give a street address in this round.

- **Start**: no runner may start more than 10 minutes after the official gun.

## Entry, fees and standards

- **Window**: early access on 26 and 27 October 2026 was limited to runners with a UTMB Index; public registration opened at 10:00 (UTC−3) on 28 October 2026 and closes on 10 August 2027 or when full.
- **Standards**: PTR 100 requires a valid UTMB Index in the 50K category; PTR 55 requires a 20K index; PTR 34, 25, 17, NIGHT 10 and RUN 7 have no index requirement.
- **Finisher benefits**: PTR 100 finishers earn a UTMB Index and 3 Running Stones; the official page states that a **finish under 26 hours qualifies for the Western States lottery**. The 30-hour race limit is not the 26-hour qualification line, so anyone targeting Western States needs a faster plan.
- **Fees (BRL current / final)**: PTR 100 1,488 / 1,600; PTR 55 1,144 / 1,300; PTR 34 792 / 900; PTR 25 660 / 750; PTR 17 528 / 600; NIGHT 10 396 / 450; RUN 7 264 / 300.
- **Fees (USD current / final)**: PTR 100 298 / 338; PTR 55 242 / 275; PTR 34 168 / 190; PTR 25 140 / 159; PTR 17 112 / 127; NIGHT 10 84 / 95; RUN 7 56 / 63.
- **Changes and transfers**: distance changes and bib transfers close on 10 August 2027. Transfer fee is R$100 (US$20 / EUR20); downgrades are not refunded.
- **Refunds**: if the organiser cancels, 60% is refunded up to four months before the race, 30% up to two months, nothing after that. The rules for a runner's own withdrawal were not verified clearly in this round, so no guess is given.

## Travel and accommodation

The official access page gives the framework: Paraty is about four hours by car from both São Paulo International Airport (GRU) and Rio de Janeiro International Airport (GIG); it lists 269 km from São Paulo and 263 km from Rio. The options are bus, rental car or private transfer, and the page says the roads are well maintained. The official page publishes only event-arena and parking-lot map links; it publishes **no race-week shuttle timetable, departure times or hotel list**, so those remain unknown and this page does not present third-party bus times as official. Lodging in Paraty town is walkable to the historic centre, but race-week availability and prices need independent checking.

## Difficulty and who it suits

Editorial judgement, not an official rating:

- 101 km and 4,850 m with a 30-hour limit. Most climbing sits between 32 and 80 km: 32.3–50.6 km alone is +1,825 m, and 71.6–79.9 km adds another +812 m; then about 1,800 m of descent in the last 22 km. The cut-offs are generous (Taquari at 7 hours, Trutario at 20, finish at 30), so the difficulty is heat and humidity, night running and the long descent rather than the clock.

![Sunrise from Pedra da Macela: a granite ridge and low cloud above the Angra dos Reis bay; not the 2027 race, an aid station or course markings](/media/routes/paraty-brazil/paraty-pedra-da-macela-2015.jpg)

*Figure 5 | Sunrise from Pedra da Macela above the Angra dos Reis bay (11 July 2015, Rafael Defavari, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)). The course reaches the 1,797 m Macela timing point at 79.9 km; the photograph only shows the summit and its view, not the 2027 race, an aid station or course markings.*

- A 19:00 start means running through the night. Taquari's 7-hour cut-off puts most of the field there after midnight, and the biggest climb (32–50.6 km) falls in the early hours; for a 15–20-hour target, Pedra da Macela comes in the morning to midday, right as coastal heat builds. Two head torches, spare batteries, a waterproof shell and 2 L of water are weather-kit decisions, not optional extras.
- The descent is not a free ride: about 1,800 m down after 79.9 km punishes the quads and patellar tendons, especially in humidity. Without a 100 km block that includes long descents, PTR 100 is not a good first hundred.
- Suited to runners with a 50K UTMB Index and a 100 km or 50-mile finish who can handle an all-night humid run and a long descent. A finish-only target should budget 28–30 hours; a Western States lottery target must be under 26.

Retreat and risk: the regulations use departure time from the main aid stations as the cut-off, so withdrawal should be registered with staff at a main aid station. No collection-vehicle timetable from each aid station to the nearest road or Paraty town is published. The course starts in Paraty's historic centre and finishes in town, but parts of the final section may still be in the forest; do not leave the marked route to find a short cut. Medical certificates, on-course medical points and emergency numbers are governed by the 2027 regulations and race briefing; this page does not replace them.
