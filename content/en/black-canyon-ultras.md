## 1. The race and the key numbers

Black Canyon Ultras runs on the Black Canyon National Recreation Trail in central Arizona. Both distances are point to point and generally southbound, with more descent than climb: the 100K starts at Mayer High School and finishes at Emery Henderson Trailhead near New River; the 50K runs the first half of the same line and finishes at Deep Canyon Ranch in Black Canyon City.

## 2. The 100K course: the first 20 miles are too fast, the climb is late

The official course description gives the order:

- From Mayer High School, about three miles of paved and dirt road, then Antelope Creek Road onto the Black Canyon Trail (BCT) singletrack; after that the race is almost entirely on the BCT.
- Singletrack alternates with jeep road; junctions are numerous and runners must follow orange ribbons with black dots and orange flags. The line trends south but constantly bends.
- At the Black Canyon City trailhead there is a short out-and-back: turn left into the aid station, turn around, keep left and continue south. The 100K must complete it; skipping it means no result.
- The route crosses creeks several times, the largest at **mile 34**. Water depends on rain; when flowing, a separate line of stepping stones is marked.
- The first 20 miles are mostly descending and fast; the official page explicitly warns not to blow up there. **Miles 28–45** repeatedly cross Black Canyon, concentrating the climbing and technical descending.
- Most of the 100K's climbing, steep descent and rocky technical running is in the final third; the course is harder than the elevation profile suggests.

![A trail corridor near Black Canyon City: a tall multi-armed saguaro in the foreground, then a dry wash, green scrub and rocky slopes. This 2023 location photograph shows the Sonoran Desert setting of the 100K's middle miles; it is not the course line or a race-day scene](/media/routes/black-canyon-ultras/black-canyon-trail-near-bcc-2023.jpg)

The route leaves the main BCT at three points: Bumble Bee Ranch, Deep Canyon Ranch (rejoining via Kay Mine Road) and the Black Canyon City out-and-back. The BCT itself is about 80 miles long, established by the Department of the Interior in 1919 as a livestock driveway along the old Phoenix–Prescott stage route; the race uses a 55-mile section.

## 5. Golden Tickets: top three, sliding to seventh

The WSER 2027 Golden Ticket page lists Black Canyon 100K among the six Golden Ticket races, with a top-three allocation: three men's and three women's automatic places. If a top-three runner already has a place or declines, the bid slides to fourth, fifth and **as far as seventh**; it does not go beyond seventh. Winners have two weeks to decide and register. A withdrawal before 1 May goes to the next runner in that race, no further than fifth or seventh; a withdrawal after 1 May goes to the general wait list.

The 2026 allocation is the best illustration:

- Men: Troyer, Costales and Knopp took the tickets; fourth-placed Woodward already had a WSER place, so the bid did not slide further.
- Women: Lichter and Flower took two tickets; third-placed Dower already had a place through Javelina, so the third ticket slid to fourth-placed Seidel; fifth-placed Hall also already had a place.

“Top three, with roll-down for already-qualified runners” is therefore not abstract: in 2026 the third women's ticket went to fourth. The old draft's “only to sixth” conflicts with the WSER 2027 “as far as seventh” and has been corrected.

## 6. Start, finish, shuttles and kit collection

- **Start:** Mayer High School, 17300 E Mule Deer Dr, Spring Valley, AZ 86333. The school car park is closed; runners and crew park at nearby churches/pharmacies or take the shuttle.
- **100K finish:** Emery Henderson Trailhead, 43376 N New River Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85087. No parking at the finish; crew park at the south-west corner of Anthem Outlets and take the free shuttle, about 10 minutes. Shuttles start at 13:00 on Saturday.

![The metal Black Canyon National Recreation Trail marker at Emery Henderson Trailhead, set in gravel and showing hiking, horse and bike symbols plus a motor-vehicle prohibition. This 2024 trailhead sign is not the 100K finish arch, timing mat or race-day set-up](/media/routes/black-canyon-ultras/black-canyon-emery-henderson-2024.jpg)
- **50K finish:** Deep Canyon Ranch, 18181 E Deep Canyon Rd, Black Canyon City, AZ 85324. 50K runners park there in the morning and shuttle to Mayer; the finish is the car park.
- **Saturday 100K shuttle:** the 24 November 2026 official table lists **Wave 1 at 05:45 / arrive 06:30**, **Wave 2 at 06:20 / arrive 07:05** and **Wave 3 at 06:45 / arrive 07:30**. The 20 November snapshot showed only Waves 1 and 3; do not assume from that old copy that the 07:30 wave has no bus. Miss your own bus and wait for the next one, starting in the corresponding wave.
- **After the race:** no shuttle back to Mayer; the 100K finish has a bus to Anthem, and the 50K finish is the car park.

**There is no race-day collection.** Anyone arriving late on Thursday or Friday must go to Deep Canyon by Saturday midday; 100K runners cannot wait until Saturday morning at the start.

Crew is allowed only at Bumble Bee Ranch, Deep Canyon Ranch and Table Mesa; the Emery Henderson finish is shuttle-only. Pacers are allowed only in the 100K, may join at Deep Canyon (31.9) and may switch at Table Mesa; the 50K has no pacing. A pacer must walk, one runner at a time, and may not carry the runner's kit or supplies.

## 7. Weather, equipment and medical

The official page's typical race-week conditions are lows in the 30s Fahrenheit (about 0–4 °C) at dawn, possible afternoon heat, and possible thunderstorms or snow. The 2027 page gives sunset at **18:06**; the 2026 guide says **18:12**, six minutes later. Lights: put a set in the Bumble Bee drop bag for an 18–20-hour finish, Deep Canyon for 14–18 hours and Table Mesa for 11–14 hours. A second backup set is sensible.

- **Mandatory kit:** the official page lists none, but cupless means carrying a cup or bottle, and the night sections require a light.
- **Medical:** MedStar Solutions provides cover. In an emergency call **480-269-4126**, not 911. Give bib number, callback number, location and mile, the problem and a description. Remote sections will take longer; if you can reach an aid station, go there.
- **Gates:** the BCT crosses grazing land; close every cattle gate behind you.
- **Road crossings:** the course crosses county roads; vehicles are not required to stop for runners, so look both ways.
- **Shooting ranges:** BLM shooting areas sit near Hidden Treasure, Bumble Bee and Table Mesa; gunfire may be heard, but the ranges are set back from the trail.
- **Withdrawal:** report to the aid-station captain with bib and chip/bib, and do not walk out of the course on your own; a remote station may only have transport when it closes.

## 8. Withdrawal and risk

- **Bail-out points:** Bumble Bee (19.4), Deep Canyon (31.9), Black Canyon City (37.2), Table Mesa (51.1) and the finish. Crew is allowed only at Bumble Bee, Deep Canyon and Table Mesa.
- **Main risks:** the mile-34 creek crossing, the repeated Black Canyon crossings from miles 28–45 with steep descents and rocks, the concentrated late climb, and February afternoon heat, storms or snow. The official page does not publish per-station medical cover or sweep times; this page does not invent them.
- **No finish parking:** crew cannot drive to Emery Henderson; they must use the Anthem shuttle. A withdrawing runner should not ask family to drive to the trailhead.

## 9. Entry and qualification

- **100K:** 20-hour race limit; a sub-17-hour finish is the WSER lottery qualifier. The 17-hour standard is not the race cut-off.
- **Golden Ticket:** top three men and women in 2027, sliding as far as seventh. Runners must meet WSER's male or female eligibility; a nonbinary runner seeking a ticket must designate the male or female category before the race.
- **50K:** no WSER qualifier and no Golden Ticket; 11-hour limit.
- **Caps and fees:** 1,200 for each distance; 100K $350–380, 50K $250–280; registration closes 6 February 2027 at 23:59 MT.
- **Lottery, refunds, transfers:** the official page publishes no lottery ratio, refund or transfer terms; this page leaves them blank rather than guessing.
