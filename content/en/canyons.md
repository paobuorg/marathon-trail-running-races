## What this race is

Canyons is Auburn's Western States Trail race. The 100K leaves China Wall, drops into Eldorado Canyon and climbs back onto the Western States Trail to Deadwood, passing Swinging Bridge and Devil's Thumb before a second Deadwood visit. It then descends to Eldorado Bridge, climbs to Michigan Bluff, crosses Volcano Canyon to Foresthill, follows Cal Street and the American River to Drivers Flat and Mammoth Bar, takes the Confluence Trail to No Hands Bridge, climbs to Robie Point, crosses Ann Trason's White Bridge and finishes in downtown Auburn.

## The course

The organiser's description runs in this order:

- **China Wall (0 km)**: the start at China Wall Trailhead. Mitchell Mine Rd leads down into Eldorado Canyon.
- **Deadwood (18.0 km)**: back onto the Western States Trail, past The Pump to Swinging Bridge (21.7 km) and up Devil's Thumb; then left onto Loop 6 for a second Deadwood visit (25.4 km).
- **Michigan Bluff (36.4 km)**: down to Eldorado Bridge, up to Michigan Bluff, then through Volcano Canyon to Foresthill (45.9 km).
- **Cal Street and the American River (45.9–74.0 km)**: Cal Street and the Western States Trail along the American River to Drivers Flat (74.0 km).
- **Mammoth Bar and Confluence (86.7–92.7 km)**: up to Mammoth Bar, then the Confluence Trail to No Hands Bridge.
- **Robie Point and finish (99.1 km)**: over No Hands Bridge, up to Robie Point, across Ann Trason's White Bridge and into downtown Auburn.

The terrain is California foothill oak woodland, river canyon and exposed trail. The race is in late April. The organiser gives no per-section technical grading or surface breakdown, so those are unknown. The 100K's climbing is concentrated in Eldorado Canyon, Devil's Thumb, Michigan Bluff and Mammoth Bar; no per-section gain table was published, so this page does not derive one from the total.

## Entry and qualification

The 2027 registration page gives all-in prices. Entries are non-refundable and non-transferable, with a single deferral to the next year available until 24 March 2027. The 100M and 100K qualify for the 2028 Western States: the 100M requires a finish under 35 hours and the 100K under 20 hours. The 100K Super Golden Ticket sends the top three women and men to the 2027 Western States; if a recipient is already qualified or declines, the place passes down but no runner below fifth is offered it. All finishers collect Running Stones; the 2027 100K header gives **6**, the 100M 4, the 50K 2 and the 25K 2 (the 2026 main page says 1 for the 25K, so the two official pages disagree). The Finals lottery requires at least one stone and a valid UTMB Index.

## Target pace and cut-off pressure

The official 2027 line is 100 km with a 20-hour limit. The table divides 100 km evenly by target finish time; it is an arithmetic baseline only and excludes climbing, technical trail, aid-station stops, night slowdowns and any official split plan.

| 14 hours | 8:24 | 7.14 |

| 20 hours | 12:00 | 5.00 |

Adam Peterman won the 2026 race in 8:18:47. Over 100 km that is about **4:59/km and 12.03 km/h**; over the 2026 chart's 99.1 km it is about **5:02/km and 11.92 km/h**. Both are arithmetic conversions only. The 2026 course was also rerouted, so the winner's pace is not a 2027 target.

What can be checked is the published cut-offs. Dividing each segment distance by the time between consecutive published cut-offs gives six implied average speeds: Start to Deadwood-2, 25.4 km / 6.5 h = **3.91 km/h**; Deadwood-2 to Michigan Bluff, 11.0 km / 1.25 h = **8.80 km/h**; Michigan Bluff to Foresthill, 9.5 km / 1.75 h = **5.43 km/h**; Foresthill to Drivers Flat, 28.1 km / 5.25 h = **5.35 km/h**; Drivers Flat to Mammoth Bar, 12.7 km / 2.5 h = **5.08 km/h**; Mammoth Bar to the finish, 12.4 km / 2.75 h = **4.51 km/h**.

The 8.80 km/h figure is not a pace a mid-pack runner can hold over 11.0 km with +358 / −417 m; the aid-station section already flags it as an administrative cut-off or a possible table mismatch. The other five segments range from 3.91 to 5.43 km/h, and the full 20 hours works out to exactly 5.00 km/h. The 2026 chart therefore cannot be read as a coherent pacing plan, and the 2027 chart must be re-checked in full when it appears.

## Transport and accommodation

- **Closest major airport:** Sacramento International (SMF), about 35 miles and 45 minutes via I-80 East, according to Visit Auburn.
- **Other airports:** San Francisco International (SFO) about 140 miles and 2.5 hours; Oakland (OAK) about 120 miles and 2 hours. All three have rental cars.
- **Local:** downtown Auburn is walkable, but Visit Auburn recommends a car for trailheads; the China Wall start is outside town. The 2026 schedule lists the first 100K shuttle at 02:45 and the last at 03:45 from Gold Country Fairgrounds Parking; the 2027 schedule is not published, so this page does not treat the 2026 times as a 2027 plan.
- **Accommodation:** Auburn and the I-80 corridor have hotels. The 100K starts at 05:00, so staying in Auburn avoids a pre-dawn mountain drive; the finish is downtown Auburn, which makes bag drop simpler than a point-to-point.

## Weather and season

Open-Meteo's Auburn town record for 24–25 April, 2015–2025 (22 days):

- Daily maximum 13.1–28.6 °C, median 18.8 °C; daily minimum 5.9–14.0 °C, median 8.6 °C.
- 5 of 22 days had at least 1 mm precipitation; 3 had at least 5 mm.

This is the Auburn town grid, not China Wall, Eldorado Canyon or the American River bottom. Late April can be much hotter in the canyons; a 05:00 start needs a light shell and headlamp, while the afternoon needs sun protection and salt.

## Withdrawal and risk

- **Crew-accessible points:** the 100K chart lists Deadwood-2, Michigan Bluff and Foresthill; the 100M chart also lists Drivers Flat, Cool-1 and Cool-2. The 100M points are not copied onto the 100K.
- **Drop bags:** the 100K takes bags only at Foresthill and Drivers Flat; the chart does not mark a separate finish bag, so that remains unknown.
- **Risk:** repeated steep climbs and descents in Eldorado Canyon and Devil's Thumb, exposure on the American River section, technical Cal Street at night, and possible wet rocks after April rain. The official page gives no per-segment technical grade; this page describes the course rather than inventing an official rating.
- **Withdrawal:** report to the aid-station captain and follow the organiser's instructions for handing in bib/chip; do not leave the course and hitch a ride. The 100K sweep and return plan was not read, so it remains unknown.
