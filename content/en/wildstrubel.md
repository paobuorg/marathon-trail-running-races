## What this race is

Wild 110 is the flagship of Wildstrubel by UTMB. It starts in Crans-Montana, Valais, climbs to Varneralp and drops to Leukerbad, crosses the Gemmi pass (2,268 m) into the canton of Bern, passes Kandersteg, Ueschine, Adelboden, Sillerebüel, Lenk and Iffigenalp, reaches the highest point at Cabane Wildstrubel (2,800 m as published), crosses the Rawyl pass (2,427 m) and returns along the Tseuzier lake and the Ro bisse to Crans-Montana.

## The course

The official route description, in order:

- **Crans-Montana (0 km)**: the Valais start, climbing to Varneralp (12.9 km). This section is run at night, and the first published cut-off is at Leukerbad (22.8 km).
- **Leukerbad (22.8 km)**: a roughly 900 m descent into town. The organiser lists this as an assistance point.
- **Gemmi pass (27.5 km, 2,268 m)**: about 987 m of climbing in 4.7 km from Leukerbad, one of the steepest sections. Crossing the pass enters the canton of Bern.
- **Kandersteg (43.6 km)**: a long descent into Kandersteg, also an assistance point.
- **Ueschine (49.4 km) → Adelboden (60.0 km)**: via Bunderchrinde and Louwene (published as water only) to Adelboden, which the organiser lists with hot food, rest, showers and a drop bag.
- **Sillerebüel (69.3 km) → Lenk (78.0 km)**: more Bernese Oberland rolling terrain; Lenk is another assistance point.
- **Iffigenalp (86.4 km) → Cabane Wildstrubel (92.1 km)**: the highest section, published as 2,800 m; TrailSplits repeats 2,788 m from the 2026 data, and both are reported.
- **Rawyl pass (2,427 m) → Tseuzier (102.7 km) → Crans-Montana (113.7 km)**: back into Valais, along the Tseuzier lake and the Ro bisse to the start. Tseuzier is the last station with a published cut-off.

The terrain is high alpine pasture, rocky passes, glacial valleys and lakeside path. The organiser stresses semi-autonomy: the organisation does not solve mountain problems for runners; the night sections are long and the Gemmi and Rawyl passes can change quickly. The organiser gives no per-section technical grading or surface mix, so both are unknown.

## Start, finish and logistics

- Start and finish are both in Crans-Montana. The organiser runs no shuttle for Wild 110; only Wild 70, Wild 55, Glacier Marathon and Wild 25 have transport from Crans-Montana (CHF 5 reservation fee, non-refundable).
- Bib collection and drop bags: the fee includes Wild 110 drop-bag transport, and the organiser asks runners to use the UTMB World Series bag issued at check-in. The 2027 collection location and times were not obtained, so they are unknown here.
- Mandatory equipment for Wild 110 and Wild 70: pack, smartphone with Swiss roaming and LiveTrail, 15 cl cup, at least 1 litre of water, 800 kcal food reserve, two working headlamps with spare batteries, 1.4 × 2 m survival blanket, whistle, 100 × 6 cm elastic bandage, waterproof breathable hooded jacket (recommended 10,000 mm, RET 13), full-length leg cover, a warm long-sleeved second layer of at least 180 g and ID. The hot-weather kit adds sunglasses, a head-and-neck cover and SPF 50 sunscreen and raises water to 2 litres; the cold-weather kit adds a down third layer, protective glasses, a beanie, warm waterproof gloves, waterproof overpants and sturdy closed-toe trail shoes. Checks can happen at any time.
- Medical and insurance: Switzerland does not require a medical certificate, but the organiser strongly recommends regular effort testing. Every runner must hold individual accident and rescue insurance valid in Switzerland; rescue is not free.
- The fee includes the bib, timing, gifts, aid stations, Wild 110 drop-bag transport, medical assistance and post-race showers, rest, massage and meal.

## Entry and qualification

Registration for 2027 opens on 17 November 2026 at 10:00 Swiss time for runners with a valid UTMB Index for 48 hours, then on 19 November 2026 at 10:00 for everyone, first come first served, with no lottery and no waiting list. The Wild 110 fee is CHF 220–275 plus 8% administration; the minimum age is 18 (born in 2008 or earlier).

Bib transfers are possible only once a race is full, to a runner who meets the criteria, up to four weeks before the event, for CHF 20; no deferral to the next year and no transfer to another event. Distance changes are allowed up to four weeks before the event, paying the difference when moving up and receiving no refund when moving down.

Elite entries are assessed on UTMB Index within the chosen category; the exact thresholds are on the organiser's elite registration page, which was not read in detail here, so they are unknown.

## Transport

- **Nearest airport:** Sion regional airport about 30 km; Geneva 180 km, Milan Malpensa 220 km, Zurich 300 km. These are road distances from Crans-Montana Tourism, not transfer times.
- **Rail:** direct trains reach Sierre from Geneva airport, Zurich airport, Basel, Bern and Lausanne; from Sierre the funicular reaches Crans-Montana in 12 minutes non-stop or 20 minutes with stops.
- **Local:** a free shuttle runs throughout Crans-Montana; Sion station is about 45 minutes and Sierre about 35 minutes by bus.
- **Race shuttle:** Wild 110 has no shuttle from Crans-Montana; only Wild 70/55, Glacier Marathon and Wild 25 have one, with a CHF 5 non-refundable booking fee. The entry fee does not include a Wild 110 start/finish transfer; start and finish are in the same resort, so staying near the start is simplest.

## Season and weather

Open-Meteo's Crans-Montana village record for 10–12 September, 2015–2025 (33 days):

- Daily maximum 6.4–23.9 °C, median 17.8 °C; daily minimum 1.2–10.1 °C, median 6.7 °C.
- 11 of 33 days had at least 1 mm precipitation; 2 had at least 5 mm.

This is the village grid at about 1,500 m, not Gemmi (2,268 m), Cabane Wildstrubel (2,800 m) or Rawyl (2,427 m). In mid-September the passes can be near 0 °C with rain, snow or strong wind; the 23:00 start makes the night feel colder. The mandatory kit — waterproof shell, full-length legs, a 180 g warm layer and two headlamps — is set for pass weather, not village weather.

## Withdrawal and risk

- **Withdrawal points:** Leukerbad, Kandersteg, Adelboden, Lenk, Iffigenalp and Tseuzier all have road/village access; Adelboden has hot food, rest, showers and bag drop and is the largest mid-race base.
- **Risk:** fast weather changes on Gemmi and Rawyl, a long night, and the 2,800 m high section after Iffigenalp. The official page stresses semi-self-sufficiency; the organiser does not solve the mountain for you.
- **Medical:** the official table marks a medical point at Col du Rawil; other stations' medical cover was not read point by point. Swiss rescue is not free, and every runner must carry valid personal accident and rescue insurance for Switzerland.
- **Withdrawal:** report to the checkpoint captain and follow instructions for handing in bib/chip; no unified sweep or return plan is published, so it remains unknown. Do not walk down the road on your own.

## Training transfer

Wild 110 is a 113 km, 6,600 m+, 30-hour Alpine loop across the Valais and Bernese cantons, from about 1,065 m to the official high point at 2,800 m, with the Gemmi and Rawyl passes and one full night out. The table breaks the demands into trainable abilities; it lists directions, not an official plan.

| Long-duration fuelling | 12 main stations, semi-autonomous | Train station-to-station eating and the 800 kcal self-carried reserve per [fueling long runs](/en/knowledge/long-run-fueling/) |
