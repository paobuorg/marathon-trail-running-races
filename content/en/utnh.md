## What this race is

Ultra-Trail Ninghai began in 2013. Its course follows the mountaineering trails of western Ninghai County through primary forest, streams, bamboo groves and ridges. The 2026 edition is title-sponsored by KOLON SPORT and is part of the UTMB World Series, with three races.

This is the organiser's 2026 data, not an estimate. Three things matter:

- **CP2 is the checkpoint that moved this year.** The 14 September update says CP2 went from Wailiao at 16.4 km to Liliao at 18.6 km and its cut-off was pushed back 30 minutes. The CNH-60 table still has CP2 at Wailiao, 16.4 km, so the adjustment applies to UTNH-100 only.
- **Every wave shares one cut-off.** The FAQ states that each checkpoint has a single cut-off calculated from the last wave's start. UTNH-100 has one wave; YNH-25 has three, which is why its start exit cut-off is 7:45.
- **Timing is at the exit.** The timing points are at the aid stations and runners must leave before the cut-off. Runners still in the station after it are cut and must go through a passive withdrawal there. Start, finish and every aid station have a timing point, and a missing split cancels the result.

## Aid, support and bags

- **Aid:** the regulations say stations provide water, sports drink and food, and require runners to leave a station carrying enough for the next one. There is no per-station food list. Each aid station has a feeding area, a rest area, timing and medical.
- **Support:** UTNH-100 runners may apply for one supporter pass. Holders may enter the support zones at CP3, CP6 and CP9 only, and may hand over food and drink only. No outside support is allowed at other stations.
- **Rest:** UTNH-100 runners may rest at CP8 Liliao and CP9 Fengshan, where camp beds are provided in limited numbers.
- **Bags:** UTNH-100 runners may deposit two 30-litre bags at the start (one for the finish, one for the change point). CNH-60 and YNH-25 runners may deposit one 30-litre bag. Only the organiser's bags and stickers may be used. The start desk opens one hour before the race, bags are collected with the bib, and finish bags must be picked up by 14:00 on 15 November.
- **A bag risk the organiser states:** because of logistics, the organiser cannot guarantee that a UTNH-100 runner who withdraws will find their CP6 bag already back at the West Gate finish. Anyone putting critical kit in the change-point bag should know this.
- **Sweeping:** only stations with cut-offs have sweep vehicles, and the organiser does not guarantee immediate transport to the finish; capacity depends on weather and on it being night.

## Packet pickup, schedule and transport

- **Pickup:** CNH-60 on 12 November 14:00–22:00; UTNH-100 on 13 November 10:00–21:00; YNH-25 on 14 November 10:00–21:00. All at the West Gate in Ninghai. Bring the original ID used to enter and the full race kit. Late pickup means losing the place. Runners entered in more than one race may collect all packs at once.
- **Briefing:** the opening ceremony, elite meeting and race briefing are on 12 November, 17:30–18:30, at the West Gate.
- **Awards:** CNH-60 on 13 November at 16:30; UTNH-100 and YNH-25 on 15 November at 10:00.
- **Getting to Ninghai:** the organiser recommends the train to Ninghai station and buying tickets 14 days ahead because of race-week demand. Drivers navigate to the West Gate; traffic controls apply around it during the event.
- **Start and finish transport:** the West Gate is packet pickup, expo, start and finish, so there is no race-morning transfer. On race day the organiser runs shuttles to the West Gate from five hotels: Sheraton Ninghai, Ninghai Xiakeju Tianming Lake Park, Ninghai Jinhai Kaimingdu, Ninghai All Seasons and Ninghai New Century.
- **Return and parking:** the official transport page gives no post-race shuttle timetable, so this page does not invent one. The finish is the start, and runners leave on their own.

## Entry and refunds

- All three races opened on 8 April at 10:00, closed on 22 April at 18:00 and drew lots on 29 April 2026.
- Fees: UTNH-100 CNY 1,380, CNH-60 CNY 780, YNH-25 CNY 480.
- Age: 18–63 for UTNH-100 and CNH-60, 18–65 for YNH-25.
- Qualification: UTNH-100 and CNH-60 require proof of one finishing result of 50 km or more, or two of 40 km or more, within the past year (obtained after April 2025), plus a medical report issued within one year by a tier-2 hospital or a professional clinic, including ECG and blood pressure, uploaded one month before the race. YNH-25 has no finishing requirement.
- Refunds: 90% until 15 August 18:00, 50% from then until 8 October 18:00, nothing after 8 October but the race pack (without bib) can be posted. Places cannot be held for the next year, the race distance cannot be changed, and transferring a bib is a violation.
- One runner may enter two or all three races. There is no grand-slam category or grand-slam award.

## Difficulty (editorial judgement)

This is not an official grading. It is this page's reading of the official distances, gains, cut-offs and course description.

- **UTNH-100 is generous early and tight late.** The first 50.5 km get until 16:50, about 10 hours 45 minutes; the remaining 50.1 km get about 13 hours 10 minutes, including the 10.9 km from CP7 to CP8 and the 9.2 km from CP8 to CP9 at night. Average pace is meaningless here; the CP6 and CP7 exit times decide the race.
- **Nearly 5,000 m of climb, concentrated in a few long climbs.** The official table shows roughly 1,000 m between CP4 and CP5 over 13.6 km, and another long climb between CP2 and CP3 over 10.6 km. The first half cannot be run at flat-road effort.
- **The CP2 move changes the opening.** The first leg is now 18.6 km instead of 16.4 km, the CP1–CP2 gap is 9.4 km, and the cut-off moved by only 30 minutes. Pacing plans built on the old distance are wrong.
- **Night and colder weather.** November nights in the Ninghai hills are colder than the old October date. UTNH-100 runs through the night, and the organiser lists the warm long-sleeve top and a spare headtorch as race kit, not as suggestions.
- **CNH-60 and YNH-25 are not simply shorter versions.** CNH-60's CP3 at Baishalinchang has no cut-off in the official table (marked N/A), and YNH-25 has only three timing points, leaving less room for error.
